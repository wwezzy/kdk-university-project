BEFORE YOU APPLY

Bring your ambition.
Prepare the essentials.

General requirements for bachelor’s applicants:

  • Completed secondary education
  • Valid identification document
  • School certificate or diploma and transcript
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Entrance examination results where applicable
  • English proficiency for English-taught programs

More questions? Contact us →
What documents do I need?

Identification, proof of secondary education, an academic transcript and a photograph. Examination and English-language results may also be requested.

How long are the programs?

All six listed bachelor’s programs take four years and are taught in English.

Are scholarships available?

Explore Academic Excellence, KDK Talent and Social Support options below. All amounts and eligibility descriptions belong to this fictional student project.

Does this form submit an official application?

No. It saves an information request in this browser. No data is sent to a university and no admission decision is made.

Where can I find my saved request?

Log in to your applicant profile using the same email address you entered in the request. It is available only in the browser where it was saved.
