HOW TO APPLY

Admission Process

  1. Choose Your Program Select the degree that matches your interests and career goals.
  2. Prepare Your Documents Collect your school certificate, transcript and identification documents.
  3. Submit Your Application Complete the application and provide the required information.
  4. Complete Admission Requirements Provide examination results or English proficiency where required.
  5. Receive Your Decision Successful applicants receive an admission offer and enrollment instructions.

REQUIREMENTS

What You Need

General requirements for bachelor's degree applicants:

  • Completed secondary education
  • Valid identification document
  • High school certificate or diploma
  • Academic transcript
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Entrance examination results where required
  • English proficiency for English-taught programs
